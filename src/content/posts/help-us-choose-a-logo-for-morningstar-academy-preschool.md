**NO CHEATING ….**

After you choose A or B above, AND … leave a comment 🙂 you can see all the logos we have to choose from on the LogoTournament Site.

[http://www.logotournament.com/contests/morning\_star\_academy](http://www.logotournament.com/contests/morning_star_academy "http://www.logotournament.com/contests/morning_star_academy")

OK … I decided to open a [HIT on Mechanical Turk](https://www.mturk.com/mturk/welcome), and for $5.00 I was able to solicit 100 responses. The bad news here, is the general population seems to be split down the middle. Out of 100 votes, 51 people liked A, and 49 people liked B. Here are their responses.
