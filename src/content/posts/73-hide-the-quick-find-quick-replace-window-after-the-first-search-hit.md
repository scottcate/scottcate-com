Let’s say you are searching for some text and you already know about [F3 and Shift+F3](/tricks/069), so you want the **\[CTRL\]+\[F\]** Quick Find or the **\[CTRL\]+\[H\]** Quick Replace window to disappear after the first search. Go to Tools | Options | Environment | Find and Replace page, and check the Hide Find and Replace window after a match is located for Quick Find or Quick Replace.
