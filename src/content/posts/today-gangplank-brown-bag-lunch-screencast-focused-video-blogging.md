I’ve been forced into @GangPlank, and I’m scared!

Not really, but kinda!

[GangPlank Headquarters](http://gangplankhq.com/) is a [very community oriented](http://gangplankhq.com/what-is-gangplank/) tech group in Mesa, and I’ve heard about all the community events they do, but I always seem to be to busy to get over there. With all the community stuff I do, it’s hard to get to everything. Anyway, in December I got a mail from [Derek](http://derekneighbors.com/) that basically said “You’re speaking in February”. Didn’t ask, just appointed me. There was an out clause, but I agreed, and really liked the idea.

Had he just called out for speakers, I (and presumably others) wouldn’t have responded.

 So Derek — Good job on the take action / [just ^ do it (NSFW)](http://tr.im/ekw7) / plan.

In January 2009, I started a new blog, and I’m focusing a lot on screen cast videos. There is a lot of reasons, but mostly I just like video. You can say and do so much more, in so much less time, and people really _Get It_ on video.

Here are my starter topics / bullet points to cover in my 20-30 minutes.

Some of these I know, and some of them I don’t. I’ll share what I’ve learned over the last 30 days in getting started with a ScreenCast video blog, as well as be asking questions about the parts I don’t know.

-   Software used / [Camtasia Recording](http://www.techsmith.com/camtasia.asp) / [Snagit](http://www.techsmith.com/screen-capture.asp) (I heart [TechSmith](http://www.techsmith.com/) Tools)
-   Screen size (why I chose the somewhat weird 900X580 resolution)
-   Microphones and pre-amp ([I use a Mobile PRE USB](http://www.m-audio.com/products/en_us/MobilePreUSB.html))
-   Rendering (Camtasia and/or Microsoft Expression Media encoder)
-   Mobile rendering (for playback on Windows Mobile)
-   Hosting / storage (why I have two storage facilities)
    -   Maybe touch a little of [Silverlight Hosting](http://silverlight.live.com/) vs. [Amazon CloudFront+AWS](http://aws.amazon.com/cloudfront/)
-   Blogging engine (how/why I chose [GraffitiCMS](http://graffiticms.com/))
-   Playing back on Silverlight vs. Flash
-   Online media players, ScreenCast.com, Youtube, Yahoo, Google, Soapbox
