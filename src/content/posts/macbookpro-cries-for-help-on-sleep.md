As I closed the lid on my MBP today, it let out a very loud and annoying cry for help. Like it was saying … _**Nooooooo, Don’t close me, I’m not ready to sleep!**_ This the first time it happened, and luckily I was at [Gangplank HQ](http://gangplankhq.com/) and [peeps](http://twitter.com/casademora/) were around to grab a quick video. All in fun …. A quick shut down and restart seems to have solved the issue, but we’ll see what happens over the next couple days/weeks. Wish me luck.
