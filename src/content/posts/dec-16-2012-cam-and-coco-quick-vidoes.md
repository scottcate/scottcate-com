# Cameron and the Lock

Cameron has been playing with this little pad lock, that we didn’t know the combination to. Last night, I found it. With only 999 combinations, I started at 000 and moved on. in about 10 minutes, I found the combination of 777. Before Cameron went to bed, i showed him it was open, but I didn’t share the code. I did tell him the answer was 1000-223. This morning he found some paper, and was figuring out the answer. Perfect time for me to turn on the camera 🙂
