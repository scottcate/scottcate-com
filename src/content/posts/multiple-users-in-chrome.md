As of last week, if I need to use two different accounts, on the same site, in chrome I would use the Primary browser, and the incognito instance. This gave me two instances of Chrome, where the cookies wouldn’t fight each other. Last week, while at lunch with guys from the office, they told me Chrome now has User Accounts. WOW. This is such a great feature, it’s worth a quick screen cast.
